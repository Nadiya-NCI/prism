## 1. Positioning (one sentence)
**"Prism is the research copilot for investors who are underwater and tired of the hype — every figure sourced, live, and honest."**

## 2. Campaign concept — *"See Clearly."*
Anti-noise, anti-hype, anti-panic. Where the sector screams "BUY THE DIP", Prism says *"let's look at your numbers."* The product IS the trust mechanism, so the ads behave like the product: sourced figures, timestamps, zero promises.

## 3. Rollout — three channels with drafted copy
**A. Social (X / LinkedIn), 3-post sequence, first week**
1. "Your portfolio is −61% vs what you paid. Fear index 34. Nobody on feeds is showing you maths like this. We built the calculator that does. — Prism" *(links to live watchlist card)*
2. "Every number on Prism carries a fetch timestamp. If it's stale, it's flagged. That's the whole product." 
3. "Fear 34 isn't a buy signal or a sell signal. It's weather. Plan around it, don't react to it." 

**B. Email, one welcome sequence (2 sends)**
- Send 1 (Day 0): *Subject:* "Your copilot just read your watchlist" — 3-line value: live valuation, sourced figures, research (not advice) boundary.
- Send 2 (Day 3): *Subject:* "What 'Research Support, Not Advice' actually means" — trust-building explainer + compliance caption.

**C. Landing page copy (on the prototype itself)**
- Hero: "**Prism** — live crypto research, an honest copilot, and zero crystal balls."
- Sub: "Top-10 market data and your portfolio's live valuation, refreshed from CoinGecko and your own sheet. Ask anything — answers are grounded in what you see. Research support, never financial advice."
- CTA: "Meet the copilot ↓"

## 4. Conversion funnel
Impress → 1 page load (live table renders) → 1 watchlist valuation → 1 copilot question → repeat session (data refreshed at 120s). KPI: ≥ 2 sessions/week; metric of *trust*: % of sessions that ask the copilot a question.

## 5. Compliance guardrails (drafted into every asset)
- Output contains no instruction verbs — "hold/trim/rebalance" appear only as *questions* the product helps users ask themselves.
- Every ad cites live-sourced figures with timestamps; no price predictions.
- Footer on all assets: "Prism provides research support, not financial advice. Figures are live-sourced (CoinGecko, Google Sheets, Alternative.me)."
- Campaign tone is calmer-than-market by design — authenticity is the launch differentiator.
